# BuildFlow AI

## Product Vision

### Vision Statement

> **BuildFlow AI empowers construction teams to plan, manage,
> collaborate, and deliver projects faster through intelligent project
> management, AI-assisted quantity surveying, real-time collaboration,
> and data-driven decision making.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Mission

To reduce delays, cost overruns, communication gaps, and inefficient
workflows across construction projects by providing one intelligent
platform for everyone involved in project delivery.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

BuildFlow AI becomes the operating system for construction companies.

Instead of using:

-   Excel
-   WhatsApp
-   Email
-   Trello
-   Google Drive
-   Separate accounting software
-   Manual BOQs

...companies use one connected platform.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Problem Statement

Construction companies struggle because information is scattered across
multiple tools.

Typical workflow today:

-   Excel for BOQs
-   WhatsApp for communication
-   Google Drive for drawings
-   Email for approvals
-   Paper site diaries
-   Manual procurement tracking
-   Separate accounting systems

This causes:

-   Delays
-   Cost overruns
-   Poor communication
-   Lost documents
-   Duplicate work
-   Slow approvals
-   Inaccurate forecasting

BuildFlow AI solves this by centralizing everything into one platform.

# Architectural Decision

**We'll build BuildFlow AI as a scalable Turborepo monorepo from day
one**, even though we're starting with a single web application.

This decision will make it straightforward to add:

-   A mobile app
-   Shared UI libraries
-   Shared configuration
-   Additional services
-   Future applications

without restructuring the project later.

This is the kind of forward-thinking architecture that distinguishes
enterprise software from small personal projects.
